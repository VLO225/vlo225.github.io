# About

This is the archive of my X account, [@VLO225](https://x.com/VLO225). Threads disappear into the timeline fast, so I keep them here in one place: full text, every chart, and the sources behind them, searchable and easy to link.

I write about defense and military affairs: weapons programs and production, force structure, ongoing conflicts, and the history behind them. Now and then I dig up something from the archives, such as an old document, photograph or report, and those go here too.

## How the archive is organised

Every entry gets a file number by type:

- **TH**: threads, kept post by post with a link back to the original on X
- **CH**: charts, with notes on the data and where it came from
- **HX**: historical finds such as documents, photographs and archival material, placed on the [timeline]({{ '/timeline/' | relative_url }})

You can search everything, filter by type or tag, and follow new entries through the [RSS feed]({{ '/feed.xml' | relative_url }}).

## Sources and corrections

I cite sources on every entry where I can. If you spot an error or have a better source, reply on X or message [@VLO225](https://x.com/VLO225). Corrections are welcome.

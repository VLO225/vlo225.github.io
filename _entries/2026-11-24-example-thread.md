This is an example entry, so delete it once you've added your own. Each paragraph becomes one numbered post: this is 1/5.

Paste your thread with a blank line between posts. The site numbers them for you and shows the total.

Put the link to the original tweet in the "link" field and a "View original on X" button appears at the top.

Add a cover image (a chart or map from the thread) and it becomes the preview card when you share this page on X.

Use tags to group related work, and the "series" field to chain multi-part threads together with part 1 of 3 navigation.
